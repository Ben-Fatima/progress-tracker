CapCut Pro Desktop – Basic Clean Reel Workflow

One-page checklist for turning raw footage into a clean vertical reel (TikTok / Instagram Reels).

Project setup

  1. Open CapCut Desktop and click New project.
  2. In the project settings, choose a **vertical 9:16** aspect ratio (e.g. 1080 × 1920).
  3. Choose a frame rate that matches your footage (typically 30 fps or 60 fps for reels).
  4. Save the project with a clear name like "2026-09-27-product-tip-reel".

Editing steps

  1. Import media – Click Import and add your clips, music, and any overlay footage (e.g. animated character MP4).
  2. Lay down the base clip – Drag your main footage onto the **bottom video track** on the timeline and trim the in/out points.
  3. Clean up the pacing – Split and delete dull sections so there is **no dead time** in the first 3 seconds.
  4. Add background music – Drag a music track to an **audio track**, lower the volume so your voice (if any) is clearly audible.
  5. Add text – Use a short hook at the beginning (1–2 lines, large font, high contrast) and minimal supporting text later.
  6. Add overlays (optional) – Place any animated character or extra video on a **track above** the main footage, then resize and position it.
  7. Review on the timeline – Play the full reel once and adjust cuts, levels, and text timing until it feels smooth.

Export settings for TikTok & Instagram Reels

  • Resolution: 1080 × 1920 (Full HD, vertical 9:16).
  • Frame rate: 30 fps (or match your footage; 60 fps is fine for very smooth motion).
  • Format: MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio).
  • Bitrate: Use CapCut's default "recommended" bitrate for social media or choose a medium-high preset.

Export a short test version first if you are trying new overlays or animated characters, then export the full reel once you are happy.
