## AI avatar / animated character tools (for MP4 overlays)

The tools below generate **talking avatar / animated character videos** that can be exported as MP4 files, then imported into CapCut as overlay tracks.

9. **Kapwing – AI Avatar Maker**  
   URL: https://www.kapwing.com/ai/avatars  
   Type: SaaS product page & docs.  
   Use: Create AI avatar videos from text or scripts and export **MP4 up to 1080p**. Useful for generating a talking character that can be dropped into CapCut as an overlay.

10. **HeyGen – AI Presenter Video**  
    URL: https://www.heygen.com/tool/ai-presenter  
    Type: SaaS product page & docs.  
    Use: Generate studio-style presenter videos from a script, then **export MP4**. Best for more realistic presenter characters delivering scripts for reels.

11. **JoggAI – Talking Avatar Video Creator**  
    URL: https://www.jogg.ai/features/talking-avatar/  
    Type: SaaS product page & docs.  
    Use: Create talking avatar clips from text with built-in editor, then export video to use in CapCut as a character overlay.

12. **CreatorKit – AI Avatar Video**  
    URL: https://www.creatorkit.com/ai-avatar-video  
    Type: SaaS product page & docs.  
    Use: Generate high-quality avatar videos for ads and social content; export **vertical MP4 up to 1080p** for TikTok/Instagram.

13. **Fliki – AI Video Generator with Avatars**  
    URL: https://fliki.ai/features/ai-video-generator  
    Type: SaaS product page & docs.  
    Use: Generate short AI videos (with avatars, captions, and voiceover), then **export MP4** in vertical formats (9:16) ready for TikTok and Reels.
