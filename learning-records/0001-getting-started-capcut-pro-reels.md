## Context

I am starting from an intermediate-beginner level in video editing (some basic experience in simple apps) and I want to become fluent in making clean, minimal reels for TikTok and Instagram using CapCut Pro on Windows 10. Animated characters and AI tools will be part of the style, but only after the base editing workflow feels natural.

## Planned learning outcomes for Lesson 0001

After completing Lesson 0001, I should be able to:

1. Create a **vertical 9:16 CapCut project** suitable for TikTok/Instagram Reels.
2. Import clips, place them on the timeline, and perform basic **cutting and trimming** to remove dead time.
3. Add **background music** on an audio track and balance its volume against the main audio.
4. Add minimal **on-screen text** (especially a short hook at the start) in a clean, readable style.
5. Export a reel as an **MP4 in 1080 × 1920** that looks correct on a phone.

## Early design decisions

- **Aspect ratio & resolution:** Standardize on **9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920**, so every reel is ready for TikTok and Instagram Reels without extra resizing.
- **Length:** Aim for **8–25 seconds** for most practice reels while learning, to keep editing focused and reduce overwhelm.
- **Style:** Prefer a **minimal, clean look** with limited text and effects; focus on pacing, clarity, and readability first.
- **Animated characters:** Treat animated characters as **overlay tracks above the main footage**. They will be introduced after the core workflow is solid, so they do not create confusion.
- **AI tools:** Start with AI avatar tools that can export **MP4 overlays** (e.g. Kapwing or HeyGen) rather than trying to animate everything inside CapCut.
