## Early design decisions

- **Spoken rehearsal, not silent reading** — the interview is verbal; silent prep builds fluency illusion, not storage.
- **Answer format:** Claim → Reasoning → Example, matching what the AI interviewer grades (structure, reasoning aloud, research judgment, English).
- **STAR story chosen:** NL 30% ruling research — real, recent, shows source hierarchy and conflict resolution.
- **Self-score rubric included** — 5 checks; ≤3 means rerun the weakest round.
