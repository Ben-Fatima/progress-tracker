# Mission: Clean, Simple Reels with Animated Characters (CapCut Pro on Windows)

## Why I am learning this

I want to learn video editing in **CapCut Pro (Desktop, Windows 10)** so I can create **clean, simple Instagram Reels and TikTok videos** that feel professional but are easy to produce.

A key part of my style is **integrating animated characters** (avatars or mascots) into my videos. These characters should support the message without making the workflow too complicated.

## Target outcome (3–6 months)

- Be able to go from **idea → script → recorded clips → edited reel → published** in under **60–90 minutes** using CapCut Pro on my Lenovo LOQ laptop.
- Use **animated characters** in at least some of my reels (for example: a talking avatar delivering my script, or a small character in the corner reacting to what I say).
- Maintain a **clean, minimal visual style**: no clutter, readable text, consistent colors, and smooth pacing.

## Current level (starting point)

- Device: **Lenovo LOQ i7, NVIDIA GeForce RTX, Windows 10**.
- Editor: **CapCut Pro (Desktop)**.
- Experience: Level **B** – I have done some simple edits in basic apps (cutting clips, maybe adding simple effects), but I am **not yet fully fluent** with CapCut’s desktop timeline, multi-track editing, or export settings.

## Constraints and preferences

- Platform focus: **Vertical short-form video** for **TikTok and Instagram Reels**.
- Workflow: I want a **simple, repeatable process** rather than lots of one-off tricks.
- Tools: I am **open to AI tools**, both:
  - features built *inside* CapCut, and
  - **external AI avatar/character tools** that export MP4 so I can import them as overlays.
- Budget: Prefer **free or low-cost tools**, but I am open to paid tools if they clearly save time or significantly improve quality.

## Learning strategy

- Build **fluency** with a small, repeatable editing workflow in CapCut Pro (import, cut, music, text, export for 9:16 reels).
- Then add **animated characters** in the simplest possible way (stock overlays or AI-generated avatar clips used as overlays).
- Use **short, focused lessons** plus quick practice tasks.
- Capture key insights in `./learning-records` and compressed reference in `./reference` so I can come back and refresh quickly.
