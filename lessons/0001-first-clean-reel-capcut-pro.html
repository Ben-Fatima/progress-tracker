Lesson 0001

First clean reel in CapCut Pro (Desktop)

From raw footage to a vertical TikTok / Instagram Reel with simple cuts, music, and text.

How this fits your mission

Your mission is to create clean, simple reels for TikTok and Instagram, on your Lenovo LOQ (Windows 10), using CapCut Pro Desktop, and eventually integrate animated characters.

This first lesson focuses on a single win: producing one finished reel from start to finish using a repeatable workflow. Animated characters will plug into this same workflow as overlays in later lessons.

Core workflow: one clean reel

Follow these steps once, slowly. The goal is not speed yet, but clarity and repeatability.

  1. Create a vertical project
    Open CapCut Desktop, click New project, and set the aspect ratio to 9:16 (vertical). If there is a resolution option, choose 1080 × 1920. This matches TikTok and Instagram Reels.
  2. Import your media
    Click Import and add:
    • Your main video clip(s).
    • Your music track (if you are using one).
  3. Lay down the base clip
    Drag your main clip onto the bottom video track on the timeline. Trim the start and end so:
    • The first 1–2 seconds already show something interesting or your main hook.
    • The total length is in the **8–25 second** range (shorter is usually better when starting out).
  4. Clean up the pacing
    Play the clip from the start. Whenever there is a pause or moment that feels slow:
    • Place the playhead just before the boring part.
    • Split the clip and delete the unwanted section.
    • Close the gap so the next moment comes in immediately.
  5. Add background music
    Drag your music track onto an audio track below the video.
    • Lower the music volume so it supports the video without overpowering your voice or main sound.
    • Fade the music in slightly at the start and out at the end if needed.
  6. Add minimal, clear text
    Add 1–2 text elements:
    • A short **hook** at the beginning (e.g. “3 tips to stop procrastinating”).
    • Optional: one more line later to highlight the main point.
    Keep the style simple: high-contrast colors, large font, no clutter.
  7. Preview the full reel
    Play the reel from the beginning:
    • Watch pacing – does anything drag?
    • Check that text is readable on screen long enough.
    • Confirm the audio balance feels comfortable.
  8. Export for TikTok / Instagram Reels
    Open the export settings and choose:
    • Resolution: 1080 × 1920 (vertical 9:16).
    • Frame rate: 30 fps (or match your footage).
    • Format: MP4.
    Export the file and note where it is saved.

For a condensed checklist of this process, see the reference document: CapCut Pro Desktop – Basic Clean Reel Workflow.

Where animated characters will fit

Animated characters (AI avatars or stock animations) will usually live on a track above your main footage:

  • You generate the character clip in an AI tool (for example, Kapwing or HeyGen) and export it as an MP4.
  • In CapCut, you import that MP4 and place it as an overlay track above your base video.
  • You then resize and position the character (e.g. in a corner) so it does not block important content.

This lesson does not require an animated character yet. The goal is to master the **base reel workflow** first so adding a character later is easy instead of overwhelming.

Practice: build your first clean reel

Practice task (about 20–30 minutes)

  1. Record or find a short clip (talking to camera or showing something on screen).
  2. Follow the workflow above to create a 10–20 second reel with:
    • Clean cuts (no long silent gaps).
    • Background music with good volume balance.
    • At least one text hook at the start.
  3. Export the reel as MP4 in 1080 × 1920.
  4. Watch the exported file outside CapCut once (like a viewer would on their phone).
